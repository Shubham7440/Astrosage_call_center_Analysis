# **Astro\_Sage Call-Center Analysis**



### ***About the Project***

In this project, I evaluated call center operations, agent performance, and channel revenue across 8,300+ customer interaction records covering 34 days and 149 support agents. My goal was to identify operational bottlenecks, understand why customers were calling back, and figure out which communication channels were actually driving revenue.



I handled the entire pipeline in Excel—from cleaning the raw logs to building dynamic models that shaped a concrete business recommendation.



##### ***What I Discovered \& Business Impact***

Found a Major Resolution Gap: I discovered that 1,275 out of 3,629 unique callers (35%) were repeat users. Even more concerning, these repeat callers accounted for 56.6% of our total call volume, signaling a clear problem with first-contact resolution (FCR) that was overloading our queue.



Mapped Channel Revenue: By breaking down revenue by support channel, I found that call-based consultations generated ₹1,68,442—making it our highest-performing channel, far outpacing chat consultations (₹45,494).



Benchmarked Agent Performance: I analyzed satisfaction scores across all 149 agents ("gurus") and spotted a massive performance gap—our top performers averaged around a 7.5 rating, while the lowest-rated agents hovered near 1.0. This gave us clear targets for who needed training versus who deserved performance rewards.



Proposed a ₹1 Crore Budget Allocation: Based on these findings, I put together a ₹1 Crore strategic investment plan split across technology upgrades, targeted agent training, and strategic hiring to fix the repeat-caller issue and boost overall customer satisfaction.



##### ***How I Built It***

Data Preparation: Cleaned and structured multi-attribute interaction logs in Excel.



Analysis \& Modeling: Used dynamic arrays, FILTER, complex logical formulas, and Pivot Tables to segment callers and calculate revenue split.
